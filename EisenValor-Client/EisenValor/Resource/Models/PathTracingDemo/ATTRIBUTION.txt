Stanford Dragon - rendering research demonstration

Source: Stanford University Computer Graphics Laboratory
Repository and usage terms: https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/
Archive: https://graphics.stanford.edu/pub/3Dscanrep/dragon/dragon_recon.tar.gz
Member: dragon_recon/dragon_vrip_res2.ply
Conversion: Tools/PathTracingDemo/import_stanford_dragon.py

The original mesh is converted to EVMH v2, normalized to unit height, centered
on X/Z and grounded at Y=0, with handedness conversion and smooth vertex normals.
The sculpture stays static; only the surrounding door is animated.

Stanford permits research use and free redistribution with source acknowledgement.
Commercial use or inclusion in a product for sale requires Stanford's permission.
Keep this attribution with the converted asset. Do not treat the model as CC0.

The Box, Sphere, Torus and LightPanel meshes are generated specifically for this
project by Tools/PathTracingDemo/generate_meshes.py. Castle props reuse the game's
existing MCastle assets and their existing materials.
